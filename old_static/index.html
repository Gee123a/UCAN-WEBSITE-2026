The Event

The Universitas Ciputra Awarding Night is an annual formal event organized by Universitas Ciputra to recognize and celebrate the achievements of students. It serves as a prestigious event to honor individuals who have excelled in various fields such as academics, leadership, entrepreneurship, and creativity.


The purpose of the Awarding Night goes beyond recognition, it aims to inspire and motivate students by showcasing excellence and dedication. By highlighting success stories, the event encourages others to strive for their own achievements and contribute meaningfully to their community.
